A high-octane 4-day mountain getaway blending pine forest serenity, snow activities, and rustic igloo village culture.

Manali, Solang Valley & Sethan Igloo Village

Old Manali – Solang Snow Point – Atal Tunnel – Sissu (Lahaul) – Jogini Waterfalls – Sethan Hampta Valley.

Duration
4D / 3N
Max Altitude
10,200 FT
Grade
Easy - Mod
Day 1: Arrival in Manali & Old Manali Heritage Walk
Check-in at our alpine cottage. Afternoon walk through cedar forests to Hadimba Devi Temple, followed by cafe hopping and live acoustic music in Old Manali.
Day 2: Solang Valley & Atal Tunnel to Sissu (Lahaul)
Drive across the engineering marvel of Atal Tunnel into Lahaul Valley. Explore the majestic frozen Sissu Waterfalls, with paragliding and snow quad-biking options at Solang Valley.

Day 4: Vashisht Hot Springs & Departure
A short morning hike to Jogini Falls, relaxing dip in natural sulfur hot springs at Vashisht village, and evening Volvo departure for your return journey.

Featured Expedition: Spiti Valley Circuit

Our flagship 7-day all-inclusive expedition through the rugged heart of the high-altitude Middle Land.

Starting ₹18,499 / person

Ultimate Spiti Valley Odyssey

Manali – Rohtang / Atal Tunnel – Kaza – Key Monastery – Hikkim – Komic – Langza – Chandratal Lake.

Duration
7D / 6N
Max Altitude
15,059 FT
Grade
Moderate
Day 1: Manali to Kaza via Kunzum Pass
Depart Manali via Atal Tunnel and Batal. Cross the formidable Kunzum La pass (14,931 ft) and descend into the cold desert capital of Kaza.
Day 2: Key Monastery & Kibber Sanctuary
Tour the 1,000-year-old cliff-hanging Key Gompa, followed by an excursion to Kibber and Chicham Bridge—one of Asia's highest suspension bridges.
Day 3: World's Highest Villages (Hikkim, Komic, Langza)
Post a letter from the world's highest post office at Hikkim, visit Komic Gompa (15,027 ft), and admire the giant Buddha statue at Langza.
Day 4: Dhankar Monastery & Tabo Mud Caves
Visit the ancient cliff-edge Dhankar Fort and the UNESCO World Heritage Tabo Monastery, famous for its preserved mud murals and meditation caves.
Day 5: Kaza to Sacred Chandratal (Moon Lake)
Trek 2 km around the pristine turquoise waters of Chandratal. Overnight high-altitude swiss dome camping under the Milky Way.
Day 6 & 7: Chandratal to Manali & Homeward Journey
Navigate the rugged Chhatru riverbeds and cross back over to Manali. Farewell dinner and homeward journey.

Essential Himachal Mountain Advisory

Key information to ensure a safe, comfortable, and well-prepared journey.

🏔️ Weather & Road Updates

Atal Tunnel keeps Manali and Lahaul accessible year-round, while passes like Kunzum and Rohtang remain weather-dependent between May and October.

📶 Mobile Connectivity

4G/5G works across Manali and Solang on Airtel and Jio. In Spiti and Chandratal, anticipate limited to zero network coverage.

🥾 Pack Smart

Carry thermal base layers, fleece jackets, sturdy waterproof trekking shoes, lip balm, and high-SPF sunscreen for mountain sun.

🚙 Reliable Mountain Transport

All our packages include professional local mountain drivers certified for snow and unpaved terrain navigation.

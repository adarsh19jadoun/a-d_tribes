Ultimate Spiti Valley Odyssey

Manali – Rohtang / Atal Tunnel – Kaza – Key Monastery – Hikkim – Komic – Langza – Chandratal Lake.

Duration
7D / 6N
Max Altitude
15,059 FT
Grade

Day 1: Manali to Kaza via Kunzum Pass
Early departure via Atal Tunnel and Batal. Cross Kunzum La (14,931 ft) with prayers at Kunzum Mata Temple before descending into Kaza basecamp.
Day 2: Key Monastery & Kibber Wildlife Sanctuary
Visit the fortified 11th-century Key Gompa. Head to Kibber (14,200 ft), known for snow leopard conservation and dramatic cliff bridges.
Day 3: World's Highest Villages (Hikkim, Komic, Langza)
Send a postcard from the world's highest post office at Hikkim (14,567 ft), explore Komic village (15,027 ft), and photograph the Buddha statue in Langza.
Day 4: Dhankar Monastery & Tabo Caves
Excursion to the cliff-hanging Dhankar Fort and the UNESCO-listed Tabo Monastery, renowned for its ancient mud stupas and murals.
Day 5: Kaza to the Turquoise Chandratal Lake
Drive back toward Kunzum Pass and trek 2 km to the sacred Moon Lake (Chandratal). Overnight alpine dome camping under the Milky Way.
Day 6 & 7: Chandratal to Manali & Homeward Journey
Traverse Chhatru and Gramphu off-road trails back to Manali. Debrief dinner and onward transfers.

Essential Spiti Mountain Advisory

Critical practical insights to ensure a safe, comfortable, and acclimatized expedition.

⛰️ AMS & Acclimatization

Spiti rests above 12,000 ft. Stay hydrated (3–4L water daily), avoid intense physical exertion on Day 1, and consult a physician about carrying Diamox.

📶 Network & Connectivity

Only postpaid **BSNL** and **Jio** work reliably in Kaza town. Expect total digital detox with zero signal at Chandratal, Kunzum, and remote villages.

💵 Cash & ATMs

The last functioning ATMs are located in Kaza, but frequently run out of cash. Carry adequate cash from Manali or Shimla for personal expenses.

🎒 Layering & Gear

Temperatures plummet below freezing at night. Pack 3-in-1 windproof waterproof jackets, thermal inners, UV protection sunglasses, and sturdy hiking boots.
